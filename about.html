Our Story

A Simple Idea. Great Food Brings People Together.

Harbour and Pine Kitchen was born from a simple idea — to create a place where exceptional food, warm hospitality, and a relaxed atmosphere come together. Inspired by the coast and the changing seasons, our menu celebrates fresh, local ingredients and timeless flavours.
